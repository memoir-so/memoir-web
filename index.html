How it works

Set it up once. Never again.

1
🔌

Install the extension

One click from the Chrome Web Store. Memoir adds a sidebar panel to your browser — it does not touch any of your existing AI tools.

2
🧠

Tell it who you are

A 3-minute onboarding: your role, what you're working on, how you want AI to respond. Memoir builds your first profile and learns more from every session.

3
⚡

Context flows automatically

Every time you open a new chat on any supported platform, Memoir injects your context before you type. Under 300ms. You just start asking.

Your context layer, always visible

A slim panel that sits beside any AI platform. You can see which memories were injected, switch profiles with one tap, and review new memory suggestions — all without leaving your chat.
